# Verbum 0.1.4

**Release notes draft — initial public release**

Verbum is a native Android Catholic Bible reader with offline Scripture, personal reading tools, and home-screen widgets.

## Included

- Three bundled offline editions covering the 73-book Catholic canon: WEBC, Douay-Rheims, and Clementine Vulgate.
- Bible search, bookmarks, highlights, personal notes, and reading history.
- Daily verse and liturgical calendar views.
- Android text-to-speech playback.
- Daily verse, daily lectionary, and compact short-verse widgets.
- System, Light, and Dark appearance, with Material You dynamic colors on supported Android 12+ devices.
- Interface resources for English, Malayalam, Tamil, Hindi, Telugu, Kannada, and Marathi.
- Translation catalog labels for source, license, language, coverage, and availability.

## Known limitations

- Complete day-by-day Mass reading citations are not bundled yet. The calendar computes liturgical dates and titles, but the reading data is incomplete.
- Some regional language downloads contain 66 books and are labeled incomplete for Catholic canon coverage.
- Some catalog sources remain unavailable while their import formats or redistribution terms are resolved.
- Lock-screen widget availability and placement depend on Android version and launcher support.
